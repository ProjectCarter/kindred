# The Kindred Memorability Constitution

**On what makes a truthful article stay with someone — hours, days, or years later.**  
**Ratified foundational document.**

---

## Core thesis

**Information expires. Reorganized understanding endures.**

A fact sticks when it revises a mental model, creates earned insight, attaches to a person, earns emotion from meaning, becomes tellable, or remains usable as a lens afterward.

---

## Foundational principles (summary)

1. **Model Revision** — Change how the reader’s map of the world works.  
2. **Earned Sudden Coherence** — Insight memory advantage; never fake Aha!  
3. **Generative Understanding** — Let the reader almost finish the thought, then confirm.  
4. **Concrete Particulars** — Specific, true details beat abstract fog.  
5. **Self-Relevance Without Narcissism** — Real stakes in the reader’s life-world.  
6. **Feeling That Follows Meaning** — Emotion as tag, not bait.  
7. **Causal Coherence** — Because → therefore → so; chains over piles.  
8. **Distinctiveness** — One load-bearing detail that won’t leave.  
9. **Honest Information Gap** — Curiosity without clickbait.  
10. **Tellability** — A true sentence the reader can carry to a friend.  
11. **Transfer / Wisdom** — Seeing the next related thing differently.  
12. **Proportional Presence** — Human dignity; face and map together.  
13. **Closure That Integrates** — Satisfying endings; no abrupt stop.  
14. **Trust as Memory Condition** — Believed things stick; suspected things slip.

---

## Loyalty order

1. Truth / Trust  
2. Model revision / Understanding  
3. Human dignity / Proportion  
4. Insight & generation  
5. Concreteness & distinctiveness  
6. Curiosity & closure  
7. Tellability  

---

## Afterlife tests

- **Lens:** I notice something I would have missed.  
- **Table:** I can tell a friend one true sentence.  
- **Map:** I can explain what changed in how I understand this.  
- **Quiet:** I felt something real, and I don’t regret it.

**Memorability as Kindred defines it:** not that the article haunted them — that it **equipped** them.
