# Kindred Editorial Constitutions

Ratified foundational law for Kindred’s worldwide newspaper.

| Document | Governs |
|----------|---------|
| [Story Standard](./STORY_STANDARD.md) | What every article must achieve before publication |
| [Memorability Constitution](./MEMORABILITY_CONSTITUTION.md) | Why truthful articles stay with readers |
| [Global Language Architecture](./GLOBAL_LANGUAGE_ARCHITECTURE.md) | Meaning vs wording; equal quality in every language |
| [Reading Experience Constitution](./READING_EXPERIENCE_CONSTITUTION.md) | What makes reading effortless, beautiful, and worth finishing |

**Enforcement in product:** digests of Story Standard, Memorability, and Global Language are applied by the Story Editor (`supabase/functions/_shared/storyEditor/`).

**Standing values:** Truth before engagement · Understanding before attention · Satisfaction before addiction · Human dignity before algorithms · Curiosity without manipulation · Learning that lasts.
