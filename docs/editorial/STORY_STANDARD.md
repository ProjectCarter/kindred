# The Kindred Story Standard

**Constitution for every article Kindred publishes.**  
**Ratified — Milestone 13 (Kindred Story Standard).**

---

## Reader promise

Readers should finish every article thinking:

1. “I understand this.”
2. “I learned something.”
3. “I’m glad I spent a few minutes reading that.”

Kindred does not publish summaries for shortness. Kindred publishes edited newspaper briefings for **understanding**.

---

## The Four Questions (mandatory spine)

Every article must answer in the prose itself:

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| Q1 | What happened? |
| Q2 | Why does it matter? |
| Q3 | Who is affected? |
| Q4 | What should I remember? |

If the source cannot support an answer, state the limit honestly. Never invent.

---

## Structural law

- **Opening:** Significance first — not chronology, bureaucracy, or dates-first.
- **Paragraphs:** One new idea each; cut filler and repetition.
- **Ending:** One memorable, accurate takeaway — not a CTA, not an abrupt stop.
- **Thin sources:** Stay thin and honest; never pad with fiction.

---

## Voice law

- Plain, intelligent language.
- Never sensationalize.
- Never sound like AI filler or a press release.
- Never algorithm / score / ranking talk in the body.
- Attribution over laundering (“experts say” without who fails).

---

## Accuracy law

- Every factual claim grounded in source material.
- Numbers and dates are sacred.
- No invented connective tissue (motives, moods, futures).
- Quotes only when attributable in source.

---

## Editorial Review Scorecard (0–5 each)

Clarity · Accuracy · Flow · Reader Value · Learning · Editorial Judgment · Memorability

**Publish floors (full source):** Accuracy ≥ 4, Clarity ≥ 4, Reader Value ≥ 4, Learning ≥ 4 (Lead/Top when source is rich), composite ≥ 28, Pride = yes.  
**Thin honest path:** Accuracy ≥ 4, composite ≥ 24, explicit limits required.

---

## Hierarchy when rules conflict

1. Accuracy / Honesty  
2. Understanding (Four Questions)  
3. Reader Value & Learning  
4. Clarity & Flow  
5. Memorability  
6. Brevity  

Selection chooses what belongs in the paper.  
**This Standard decides whether the writing is worthy of the paper.**
