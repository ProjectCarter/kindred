# Kindred Global Language Architecture

**Language is a first-class feature of Kindred — not an afterthought.**  
**Ratified foundational document.**

---

## Declaration

Kindred is a worldwide newspaper. Version 1 may launch in English only; that is launch scope, not identity.

Every article, summary, explanation, recommendation, Bandit message, notification, audio edition, and future feature must be conceived so additional languages can be added later without redesigning the product’s meaning.

---

## Core principle

**Separate meaning from wording.**

| Layer | Holds |
|-------|--------|
| **Meaning** | Facts, stakes, causal spine, honest limits, memorability intent |
| **Wording** | Locale-native headline, body, Bandit, UI, audio |

**Law:** A great translation is not one that preserves the words. It is one that preserves the **understanding**.

English is the first expression — not the “real” article with lesser children.

---

## User experience law

- Users choose preferred language in-app.  
- Onboarding may suggest device language; Settings can always change it.  
- Changing language updates the entire experience (headlines, articles, local, Bandit, notifications, audio, future AI).  
- Every supported language receives the same editorial quality.

---

## Editorial law

Preserve across languages: truth, nuance, context, emotional weight, clarity, memorability.

Avoid authoring that depends on English idioms, slang, untranslatable wordplay, or headlines that only work in English.

Headlines are **naturally rewritten** per locale — not literally mirrored.

Dates, measurements, currencies, quotations, punctuation, and formatting respect local conventions without altering the fact set.

---

## Native reader test

A careful native reader must be able to say:

1. This does not feel translated.  
2. I understand this.  
3. The emotional weight feels fair.  
4. The takeaway is memorable and true.  
5. I’m glad I spent my time reading that.

**Meaning is global. Wording is local. Understanding is sacred. Every language edition is an original.**
